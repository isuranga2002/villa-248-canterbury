Welcome to Villa 248

A private, two-storey home on the Canterbury estate

Villa 248 is a modern two-storey home in the Canterbury residence at Kahathuduwa. With 152 m² of living space, four bedrooms and three bathrooms, it suits families and groups of friends who want the room of a real home, plus direct access to the swimming pool, gym and salon.

Downstairs there's a bedroom, a bathroom, a beautifully designed dining area and a fully equipped kitchen. Upstairs are three more bedrooms, including the master with its own en-suite. There's also a pergola built for entertaining.

Questions

Frequently asked

How many people can stay?

The villa has four bedrooms and three bathrooms, which is plenty of room for a family or a group of friends. Tell us your group size when you enquire and we'll confirm the best set-up.

Can we use the Canterbury swimming pool and gym?

Yes. The villa is in the Canterbury residence, which has direct access to the swimming pool, gym, salon and more. Facility rules and opening hours are set by the residence.

Is there parking and EV charging?

Yes. There's free private parking at the villa and an electric-vehicle charging point.

How do I get there from Colombo?

The villa has easy road links to Horana (9 km), Piliyandala (13 km), Panadura, Galle Road and Colombo. Use the “Get Directions” button above to navigate with Google Maps.

Can I cook my own meals?

Yes. The kitchen is fully equipped with cookware and kitchenware. If you'd rather eat out, Sigiri Restaurants is about 1.5 km away.

How do I pay and confirm?

Send your dates using the form and the host will reply with the rate and payment details to confirm your booking.
